Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an image to pixel art online for free?

Upload your image to PixelCraft, select your preferred block size and retro color palette, then click Download PNG Sprite (4x upscale).

Is my uploaded image saved on a server?

No. PixelCraft processes images 100% inside your web browser using client-side HTML5 Canvas API for complete privacy.

Can I export pixel art for Minecraft or Perler beads?

Yes! PixelCraft includes specialized exporters for Minecraft block count blueprints (.txt) and printable craft patterns with DMC/Perler color codes.
